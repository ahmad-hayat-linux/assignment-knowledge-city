# Jobs To Be Done

A job describes what a person needs to get done whether or not any app exists. Each job names the role it belongs to (see `app-roles.md`).

## J-1 (R-1 Salaried Employee)

When my pay slip shows an income tax deduction that I don't understand, I want to check whether the amount taken is correct, so I can be confident I am not being over- or under-taxed.

## J-2 (R-1 Salaried Employee)

When I plan my monthly budget around my salary, I want to know how much I will really take home after tax, so I can commit to rent, bills and savings without a surprise.

## J-3 (R-2 Job Seeker)

When I receive a job offer quoted as a gross package, I want to know what I would actually keep after tax, so I can judge whether it is better than my current pay.

## J-4 (R-3 HR/Payroll Officer)

When I prepare a salary estimate for a colleague or an offer letter, I want to see exactly how the tax was worked out and which year's rates it uses, so I can explain and defend the figure if I am asked.

## J-5 (R-2 Job Seeker)

When I am negotiating a salary, I want to know how much of each extra rupee I would keep, so I can decide what raise is worth asking for.
