# App Roles

The app is a salary income tax estimator for salaried individuals in Pakistan (tax year 2026-27, 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027). It has no accounts or logins, so roles describe kinds of people and their situations, not permission levels.

## R-1 Salaried Employee

A Salaried Employee is a person employed by a company in Pakistan whose employer deducts income tax from their monthly pay. They think in monthly amounts and want to be sure the deduction on their pay slip is right and what they will really take home. They can enter their salary, see their monthly and yearly tax and net pay, see their effective tax rate, and see how the tax was built up slab by slab. They must never be shown a negative tax, a net pay above their gross pay, or a result made from invalid input.

**How their needs differ:** they have one known salary and want to check it and plan around it. They are not comparing figures or explaining them to anyone else.

## R-2 Job Seeker

A Job Seeker is a person weighing a job offer or a pay rise who is quoted a gross package, often as a yearly figure. They need a quick answer to "what would I actually keep?" and to try several amounts in a row. They can enter a monthly or yearly amount, switch between the two, change the amount and see every result update, and see the tax rate that applies to their next rupee. They must never mistake the estimate for an official figure, and they must never be shown a wrong result because of an unclear or invalid amount.

**How their needs differ from R-1:** they are exploring several amounts rather than checking one known salary, they often start from a yearly figure, and they care about the value of an extra rupee when negotiating.

## R-3 HR/Payroll Officer

An HR/Payroll Officer is a person who prepares salary estimates for colleagues or offer letters and may be asked to justify a figure. They need to show how the tax was worked out and which year's rates were used. They can enter an amount, see the tax in each slab, see the full slab table, and see the tax year the estimate is based on. They must never present a figure that does not match the stated slabs and tax year.

**How their needs differ from R-1 and R-2:** they do not need the answer for themselves. They need the working so they can explain and defend it to someone else.
